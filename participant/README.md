# Practical AI at Work: your folder

**To start, open `workbook/index.html` by double-clicking it.** It opens in your web browser. You need no internet connection and nothing to install, and nothing you type leaves this computer.

If double-clicking opens the file in a text editor instead: right-click it, choose *Open with*, and pick your web browser. If your computer will not open the workbook folder, open `workbook-single-file.html` instead. It is the same workbook in one file.

## What is in this folder

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| `workbook/` | The workbook: the case, five exercises and ten questions. Open `index.html`. |
| `workbook-single-file.html` | The same workbook as one file, for sending on or for computers that make folders awkward. |
| `handout.html` | Three cards on one page: the five-part brief, how to check an answer, and what must not go in. Print it and keep it by your keyboard. |
| `case/` | The case as plain files: the e-mail thread, the policy, the orders table and the eight snippets. |

## Good to know

- **Any assistant, or none.** Use any chat assistant your employer allows. If you have none, each exercise has an answer ready for you.
- **Your answers stay on this computer.** The workbook keeps them in your browser. To keep a copy, use *Save my answers as a text file* at the end of the workbook. In a private window, your answers are lost when you close it.
- **Everything in the case is invented**: the businesses, the people and the figures. Never type real customer or company data into the workbook.
