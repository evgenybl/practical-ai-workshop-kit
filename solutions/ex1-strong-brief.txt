Larkfield Wholesale (invented): returns and credits policy
For customer service staff. Read this before you answer a complaint.

1. Damage. A customer must report damaged goods within 2 working days of delivery, with photos of the goods and their packaging.
2. Missing goods. A customer must report missing goods within 5 working days of delivery. We send missing goods on our next delivery run to the customer's area, with no delivery charge.
3. Who approves a credit. Anyone in customer service may approve a credit of up to $100.00. A credit above $100.00 must be approved by the operations manager.
4. How a credit is given. A credit is given as a credit note against the customer's invoice, within 10 working days of approval. We do not pay credits into a bank account or onto a card.
5. Other costs. We do not pay for a customer's other costs, such as goods bought elsewhere, lost sales or staff time, unless the operations manager agrees in writing.
6. Never promise by e-mail. Do not promise any of these in an e-mail: a credit, or its amount, before it has been approved; a delivery day the warehouse has not confirmed; payment of a customer's other costs.
7. Replies. Answer every customer e-mail within 1 working day, even if the answer is "we are still checking".

## Task

## Form
An e-mail of at most 150 words, in three short paragraphs: first, an apology for not confirming a delivery day; second, where things stand on the missing cartons and on the credit she asks for; third, what happens next and when she will hear from me. Plain and warm, with no stock phrases. Sign it Tom Becker.

## Limits
Do not promise a credit or any amount: a credit above the limit in the policy needs the operations manager's approval. Do not promise a delivery day: the warehouse has not confirmed one. Do not offer to pay for the cups she bought elsewhere. Do not offer anything the policy does not allow, such as a discount. Use only facts from the e-mails and the policy; if something is not there, say we will find out.
