# Run sheet: the planned 90 minutes

Minute by minute: what the room does, what you do, and what is on the screen. All times are **planned**: this kit has not yet been taught to a class. What to say at each point is in [`guide.md`](guide.md).

| Block | Planned minutes |
|---|---|
| Welcome and the case, with the policy read | 8 |
| Exercise 1: From a wish to a brief | 18 |
| Exercise 2: Summarise, and prove it | 20 |
| Exercise 3: Find what is wrong (answers A and B) | 15 |
| Exercise 4: What must not go in | 20 |
| The ten questions, and the close | 9 |
| **Total** | **90** |

Exercise 5, *Make it reusable*, is homework: you set it in the close. Answer C of exercise 3 is for those who finish early, or homework.

**Keeping the case in view.** People compare answers with the thread and the policy all session: on the printed case, or in a second window of the workbook kept on *The case*. Say so in the welcome.

## Exercise 5, when it runs in the session

In the half-day variant ([`variants.md`](variants.md)) exercise 5 runs in the room, and its debrief question is: "Who in your team would use this template, and what would they need to know that is not in it yet?"

## If you are running late

- **Cut first:** in exercise 2, give everyone the recorded answer instead of waiting for assistants. About 3 minutes.
- **Cut second:** in exercise 4, part 2, demonstrate the helper on the screen instead of everyone doing it. About 4 minutes.
- **Already out of the plan:** exercise 5 and answer C of exercise 3 are homework.
- **Never cut:** the debriefs. They are where the lesson lands.
